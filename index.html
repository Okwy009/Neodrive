Fleet plans · coming soon

Put cars on the road. Share what they earn.

Plan holders fund real vehicles that we lease to vetted ride-hail and delivery drivers. Lease income, after maintenance, insurance and our fee, is paid out to plan holders. Income isn't guaranteed and you can lose money.

Not open yetPlans open country by country once we're authorised. The waitlist is free and takes no payment.
  1. 01

    You fund a vehicle

    Your plan is tied to specific cars, listed by plate and VIN in your account.

  2. 02

    A driver leases it

    Drivers are vetted, insured and pay a weekly lease. The car is tracked and serviced by us.

  3. 03

    Income is shared

    Monthly statements show lease income, every cost and your share. Nothing is paid from new deposits.

  4. 04

    The car is sold at term end

    Sale proceeds are returned to plan holders. Resale value can be lower than expected.
